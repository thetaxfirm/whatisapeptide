References

Peer-reviewed sources are cited with DOI and PubMed ID. Regulatory sources are cited to the issuing agency's own published document wherever one exists, in preference to secondary commentary. Where this guide describes a compound as lacking human evidence, that absence is itself the finding — there is no citation to give, and any source claiming otherwise should be checked against the primary literature.

A note on secondary commentary. Legal and consultancy analyses of the 2023 Category 2 additions, the April 2026 removals, and the July 2026 PCAC votes were consulted in preparing this guide and are useful for practitioners, but every factual claim about FDA action in this guide is sourced to the agency's own documents listed above. Readers are encouraged to check those primary documents directly, since this area continues to move.
