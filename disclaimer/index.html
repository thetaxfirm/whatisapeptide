Disclaimer

This guide is provided for educational and informational purposes only. It is not medical advice, it does not establish a clinician-patient relationship, and it is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified, licensed healthcare professional. Nothing in it is a recommendation, instruction or encouragement to obtain, possess or use any compound.

On approval status. Many compounds described here are not approved by the FDA or any comparable regulator for human use. Products sold as "research use only" are not authorised for human consumption, are not manufactured under pharmaceutical quality standards, and are not subject to premarket review for safety, efficacy, identity, purity or sterility. Where a compound is an approved medicine, that approval applies only to the specific indication, population and dose described in its labelling, and requires a prescription and medical supervision.

On dosing. This guide deliberately does not provide dosing information for compounds that are not FDA-approved, because no authoritative human dosing data exists for them. Doses stated for approved medicines are reproduced from approved labelling for reference and are not a recommendation for any individual.

On risk. Peptides can cause adverse effects, can interact with prescribed medications, and — when obtained outside the regulated supply chain — may be impure, contaminated, degraded, mislabelled or counterfeit. Several compounds described here are prohibited in competitive sport.

On currency. The regulatory landscape described in this guide changed substantially during 2023 and again in 2026 and continues to move. Statements about legal and regulatory status were accurate to the best of our knowledge at the time of writing and should be independently verified before being relied upon. Scientific findings described here reflect published evidence at the time of writing and may be superseded.

Please speak with a knowledgeable, licensed healthcare professional before considering any compound described in this guide.
